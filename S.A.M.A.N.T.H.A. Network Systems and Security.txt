3-Tier Network
First Tier: VLAN 10
Devices: Personal things, Computers, Phones, anything normal to connect to the outside world
Rules: allowed full internet access

Second Tier: VLAN 20
Devices: S.A.M.A.N.T.H.A.
Rules: Default Deny, explicit allow
--Whitelist select websites: (ex. weather.com) or specific search API's (ex. Google search API)

Third Tier: VLAN 30
Devices: Anything in the house that is hardware
Rules: No Internet connection, not allowed to have external connections

Possible Brands:
Ubiquiti UniFi (Expensive, updated frequently, good UI)
*pfSense (Most control, open source)
TP-Link Omada (Budget friendly Ubiquiti)

WAPs (Wireless access points) must be installed (required by larger houses)
